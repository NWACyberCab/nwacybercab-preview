For businesses

Autonomous delivery
for NWA businesses.

RoboRide plans to use the same autonomous Cybercab fleet that carries riders to move goods across Northwest Arkansas: point to point, on demand, with no courier to schedule. We're not operating yet. Tell us what you'd want to move and we'll keep you posted as plans firm up.

Use cases we're designing for

What could a driverless Cybercab carry?

These are planned use cases, not services available today. Capacity, item limits and pricing haven't been set.

Retail & local shops

Same-region delivery of customer orders from storefronts in Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale and Fayetteville.

Restaurants & catering

Takeout and catering runs between kitchens and customers, without scheduling a driver.

Business-to-business

Documents, parts and supplies between offices, warehouses, shops and job sites.

Employee rides

Getting staff between offices, campuses, hotels and XNA.

Venues & events

Pickup zones and group rides for concerts, festivals and game days.

How business delivery would work

Three steps, once we launch.

  1. Request a pickup

    Tell us the pickup and drop-off points and what's being sent.

  2. A Cybercab arrives

    Load the goods at the curb; the vehicle drives itself to the destination.

  3. The recipient unloads

    The recipient meets the vehicle and takes the delivery. Exact hand-off and verification steps will be shared before launch.

Booking tools, tracking, item limits and billing are still being designed. We'll share details before any service starts.
