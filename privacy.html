RoboRide LLC ("RoboRide", "we", "us") is a Northwest Arkansas company preparing to offer autonomous rides and goods delivery. RoboRide is not operating yet. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address,
  • what you're interested in (rides, goods delivery, business partnership, or press), and
  • the address of the page you submitted the form from.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, location, or payment information on this website.

How we use it

  • To email you updates about RoboRide's launch and services in Northwest Arkansas.
  • To follow up on business, partnership, or press interest you tell us about.

We will never sell your information.

Services that process your information

  • Formspree receives and stores waitlist submissions on our behalf. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on the homepage. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy.

The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. [TODO: update this if analytics are added later.]

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist information until RoboRide launches service, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. Until we have a business email address, you can ask us to delete your information through the waitlist form: choose any option and say in your request that you want to be removed.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your email from the waitlist, or ask what information we hold about you, at any time by contacting us. You can also ignore or opt out of any email we send.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
