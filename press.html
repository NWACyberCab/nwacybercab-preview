About RoboRide LLC

RoboRide LLC is a Northwest Arkansas company preparing to operate autonomous Tesla Cybercabs for passenger rides and goods delivery across Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale, Fayetteville and the surrounding region. RoboRide is not yet operating and is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. Learn more at www.nwaroboride.org.

Fast facts

  • Company: RoboRide LLC
  • Based in: Northwest Arkansas
  • Founder: Joseph Bowers
  • Services (planned): autonomous rides; goods and package delivery
  • Planned service area: Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale, Fayetteville, Farmington, Siloam Springs, Eureka Springs
  • Status: pre-launch; waitlist open
  • Founded: June 8, 2026
