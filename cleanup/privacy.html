Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

RoboRide LLC ("RoboRide", "we", "us") is a Northwest Arkansas company preparing to offer autonomous rides and goods delivery. RoboRide is not operating yet. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address,
  • what you're interested in (rides, goods delivery, business partnership, or press), and
  • the address of the page you submitted the form from.

If you use the business interest form on our For Businesses page, we collect:

  • your business name and email address,
  • the city you're in and your main use case,
  • any message you choose to include (optional), and
  • the address of the page you submitted the form from.

We don't ask for your personal name, phone number, precise location, or payment information on this website.

How we use it

  • To email you updates about RoboRide's launch and services in Northwest Arkansas.
  • To follow up on business, partnership, or press interest you tell us about. Business interest form submissions are used only to respond to you about business services and partnerships.

We will never sell your information.

Services that process your information

  • Formspree receives and stores waitlist and business interest form submissions on our behalf. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter with no cookies and no personal tracking, to count page visits. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist and business interest form information until RoboRide launches service, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. To ask us to delete your information, email privacy@nwaroboride.org, or use the waitlist form: enter your email and choose “Remove me from the waitlist.”

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your email from the waitlist, or ask what information we hold about you, at any time by emailing privacy@nwaroboride.org. You can also ignore or opt out of any email we send.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

RoboRide LLC, Northwest Arkansas. For privacy and data requests (access or removal), email privacy@nwaroboride.org. For anything else, email hello@nwaroboride.org.
