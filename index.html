Services

Built for every
NWA journey.

Everyday Rides

From Fayetteville neighborhoods to Bentonville's Crystal Bridges — get where you need to go without a driver.

Corporate Accounts

Employee transport between offices, campuses, and hotels. We plan to offer business accounts with centralized billing.

Scheduled Rides

Plan ahead for early-morning XNA airport runs, Razorback game days, and late-night returns.

Event Shuttles

Getting crowds to and from concerts, festivals, and game days. Group bookings are planned.

Night Owl Mode

A safe ride home from Dickson Street, the Bentonville Square, or downtown Rogers after a night out.

Goods & Package Delivery

Cybercabs won't just carry people. Local shops, restaurants, and businesses will be able to send goods across NWA. Learn more.

Goods Delivery

Moving packages,
not just people.

The same autonomous fleet that gives rides will also carry goods. RoboRide plans to offer local, same-region delivery for NWA businesses: point-to-point, without a courier.

Local Retail

Get orders from shops in Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale, and Fayetteville to customers' doors across the region.

Restaurants & Food

Takeout and catering runs between kitchens and customers, with no driver to schedule.

Business-to-Business

Documents, parts, and supplies moved between offices, warehouses, and job sites around NWA.
